# Spatial narrative architecture

The spatial narrative diagnosis has been superseded by the implemented
[IF engine](if-engine.md#places-and-ways). That guide describes place extents,
geometry-derived ways, unified actor presentation and cosmetic atmosphere.

Descriptions use disclosed facts. Authored place hints support durable names;
unhinted place identity is client-local and can change after client restart.
Do not treat the earlier proposal for client-created durable anchors as an
implemented backend feature.

The original diagnosis and proposals are preserved in the
[historical design](history/spatial-narrative-architecture.md).
