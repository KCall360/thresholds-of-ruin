# Stormlight armor comparison

## Published mundane armor

The Starter Rules armor table (p. 52) distinguishes protection from training:

| Armor | Deflect | Normal trait | Expertise effect |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Uniform | 0 | Presentable | None |
| Leather | 1 | None | Gains Presentable |
| Chain | 2 | Cumbersome [3] | Removes Cumbersome |
| Breastplate | 2 | Cumbersome [3] | Gains Presentable |
| Half Plate | 3 | Cumbersome [4] | Changes threshold to [3] |
| Full Plate | 4 | Cumbersome [5] | None |

Under the armor rules (p. 50), Cumbersome's bracketed number is a **Strength
threshold**, not a bonus. Below it, the wearer is Slowed and has disadvantage on
Speed-based tests. Slowed halves movement (p. 26). Expertise does not generally
add Deflect; Full Plate's threshold remains 5. Presentable avoids armor-related
social attention and conversation penalties. Only one armor type benefits a
wearer at once.

Deflect subtracts **impact, keen and energy** damage (pp. 14, 50). Physical
Defense is separately calculated from 10 + Strength + Speed, with applicable
modifiers, and sets attack difficulty (p. 14). These are not interchangeable
defensive statistics. Enhanced attributes do not increase defenses (p. 25).

## Comparison boundaries

The [Rogue source armor catalog](rogue-5.4.4/content.md#armor-8) owns its eight
identities and AC values. Original Rogue armor changes the chance of being hit,
not damage reduction; see its [combat rules](rogue-5.4.4/rules.md).
The [TOR item reference](../reference/items.md#equipment-birth-and-the-finite-rogue-catalog)
owns the adapted armor ladder and its provisional-balance rationale.

TOR's additive reduction is conceptually closer to Stormlight Deflect than to
Rogue AC, but it is **not a direct conversion**. In particular,
[ordinary TOR armor](../reference/items.md#armor-and-corrosion) reduces Impact
and Keen, whereas this Stormlight edition also includes energy. Magical wards
are a separate TOR contract. Different damage scales and other defenses prevent
equating protection merely because two numbers match.

Leather, chain mail and plate mail have straightforward descriptive counterparts
in Leather, Chain and Full Plate. Ring mail, studded leather, scale mail, splint
mail and banded mail have no separate rows in this Stormlight table. Treating
splint or banded armor as Half Plate would be a rough analogy, not a published
conversion. The Starter Rules permit reskinning descriptions while retaining
statistics (p. 50), but choosing those mappings for TOR would still be a new
design decision. Uniform and Breastplate have no direct entries in Rogue's eight.

The useful design distinction is protection versus tradeoffs: Stormlight's
table differentiates training, Strength requirements and social suitability,
not merely successive protection values. This observation does not authorize
adding its traits to TOR or recalibrating existing equipment.

## Shardplate evidence limit

The licensed [Demiplane Shardplate entry](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/cosmererpg/items/shardplate)
identifies it as a Stormlight Handbook item, but its detailed rules were not
available in the retrievable public text. The Starter Rules table above excludes
it; the checked official quick-start character collection did not establish its
statistics either. Do not infer Shardplate values, charges or expertise effects
from this note, or equate it with ordinary Rogue plate mail. A comparison of its
special mechanics needs accessible published Handbook rules, with edition checked.
