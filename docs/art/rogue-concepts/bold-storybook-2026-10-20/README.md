# Bold Storybook Rogue references — 2026-10-20

The maintainer supplied this set for ongoing refinement of the full Rogue cast:
26 monsters on 24 creature sheets, plus the separately named approved humanoid
body anchor. These are design references, not client renders or evidence of
implemented behavior. Ice Monster's sheet is retained for provenance; its
refinement is deferred while the maintainer reconsiders that design.

The authoritative [Bold Storybook definition](../../3d-art-direction.md#proportions-bold-storybook)
governs proportions, mature anatomy, clothing, armor, accessories and animation.
Its text takes precedence over incidental image annotations or conflicting
image details. Preserve each species' identity and use the references to improve
shared procedural generators. Labels, poses and costume suggestions do not
authorize gameplay changes or undisclosed equipment or abilities.

## Provenance and integrity

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records the reference date, provenance, exact
filename, byte size and SHA-256 of every supplied PNG. All 25 files were copied
without conversion or alteration and checked against their source hashes;
source originals were preserved. The named anchor's matching hash is recorded
explicitly. The [historical WebP index](../README.md#historical-missing-model-references)
preserves the earlier implementation references, and the
[creature-model plan](../../../plans/rogue-creature-models.md) distinguishes that
original 16-model scope from current full-cast refinement.
